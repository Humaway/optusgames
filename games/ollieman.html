OllieMan

The network went down across the CBD. Ollie has a shoulder-mounted USB‑C launcher and about four hundred glass towers to swing off. Build speed, keep off the pavement, and light the 5G masts back up.

Swing

Hold LMB to fire a cable at whatever you're looking at. It sticks to rooftops, ledges and mast tips. Let go at the bottom of the arc to convert your speed into distance.

Go faster

Hold W while swinging to reel in — a shorter cable means a tighter, quicker arc. Release with Space for a bigger launch than letting the button go.

Score

Yellow data nodes float between the buildings. Every second you stay off the ground raises your streak, and the streak multiplies everything you collect.
