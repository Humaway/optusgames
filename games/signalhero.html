    Settings

    Delay and key bindings stay put while the tab is open, and ride along in the address bar so a reload keeps them.

    Audio delay

    Nudge this up if your hits keep reading as late. Bluetooth headphones usually need somewhere between +80 and +180 ms. Works mid-song too, with [ and ].

    Not sure what to set?

    A steady click plays. Tap Space in time with it about ten times and the gap between the click and your tap becomes your delay.

    Gameplay

    Punish overstrumming

    Hitting a lane with no note there breaks your combo and costs signal, so mashing every key is worse than playing the chart.

    Key bindings

    Click a lane and press the key you want. Or use Set all and press your keys left to right in one go. The editor uses these when you record taps.
