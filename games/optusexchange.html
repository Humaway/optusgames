How the floor works

Four things worth knowing before you put money down.

Prices move on real orders. Every trade anyone places pushes the price in the direction they bet. Big orders push harder, and thin stocks move furthest.
You can bet either way. Long profits when a price rises. Short profits when it falls, and holds the position value as margin until you close it.
The wire is the edge. Some stories say what they mean. Others just tell you something happened and leave the trade to you.
Nothing moves alone. These companies are tangled together. When one drops, work out who supplies it, who competes with it, and who sells to its customers.
